# MAATRIVAANI

### Offline AI Classroom Copilot for Mother-Tongue-Based Education

MAATRIVAANI is an offline-first AI classroom copilot designed to help Hindi-medium trained teachers deliver mother-tongue-based foundational education in tribal-language classrooms.

The platform combines Hindi speech recognition, education-aware translation, tribal-language voice/text output, FLN-aligned learning content generation, and offline-first operation on low-cost Android devices.

---

## Problem Statement

**SIH 2026 – Problem Statement ID: 26042**

### AI-Powered Vernacular Pedagogy and Real-Time Translation Tool for Mother Tongue-Based Primary Education

Jharkhand's Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE) programme requires scalable support for classrooms where teachers may not be proficient in the tribal language spoken by students.

Teachers trained primarily in Hindi can face difficulties communicating lessons, activities, instructions, and assessments in languages such as Ho, Mundari, and Santhali.

MAATRIVAANI addresses this teacher-language gap through an offline AI-assisted classroom workflow.

---

## Our Solution

MAATRIVAANI is **not designed as a general-purpose translation application**.

It is an **education-specific classroom copilot** that understands the teaching context and helps convert classroom instructions into validated mother-tongue learning content.
